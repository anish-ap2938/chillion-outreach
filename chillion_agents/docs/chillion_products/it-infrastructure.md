# IT Infrastructure & Enterprise Solutions

Chillion supplies and supports enterprise IT infrastructure for government, defense, and enterprise clients across India.

## Capabilities
- HP, Dell, Lenovo, Fujitsu, and Acer enterprise hardware
- Servers, storage, networking, and office automation
- Network, server, and database monitoring
- On-site and remote AMC support with pan-India coverage
- IT project management from design through delivery

## Buyer pain points
- Multiple vendors for hardware, network, and support
- Unplanned downtime and slow incident response
- Aging infrastructure with unclear upgrade path
- Limited local support outside major metros

## Outreach angle
Position Chillion as a single accountable partner for infrastructure supply, deployment, and AMC — not just a box mover.
